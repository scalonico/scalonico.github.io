I am an econometrician at the Graduate School of Management, University of California, Davis. My research covers econometrics and statistics, with a particular interest in program evaluation and causal inference methodology, with applications to public health and public policy. My recent research focuses on estimation and inference for regression discontinuity designs, treatment effects and program evaluation methods more generally.

I have published in leading journals in economics and statistics, including Econometrica, Review of Economics and Statistics and Journal of the American Statistical Association. My work has also appeared in leading medical and public health journals, including JAMA, BMJ and The Lancet Public Health.

I am originally from Haedo, Buenos Aires, Argentina. I studied economics at the Universidad de Buenos Aires and the Universidad Torcuato Di Tella, and received my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Michigan in 2014. Before joining UC Davis, I was an Assistant Professor at Columbia University (2019–2024) and at the University of Miami (2014–2019).
